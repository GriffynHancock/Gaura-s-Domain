Crypto 101 · Module 02 · Encoding

Hidden in Plain Sight

Encoding isn't a lock, it's a costume. The same bytes can be worn as text, as an image, as base64. Nothing here is secret; you just have to recognise the disguise and take it off.

THE IDEA. Encoding (base64, hex, ROT) is reversible and needs no key — anyone can undo it. That's different from encryption, which needs a key to read. Attackers still stack encodings to dodge antivirus and slow analysts down; every layer is visible on purpose. Your job: recognise → pick the method → decode → read the flag.
