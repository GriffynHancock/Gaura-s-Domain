Encryption 101

Encryption is the art of hiding a secret so that the person you want to read it sees it immediately, while the person you're hiding it from might never find it, even if they searched for the rest of time.

ℹ In this case, the information being hidden is a string of text, and its key is a number. To use this simple method of encryption, all you have to do is convert every letter into a number between 0 and 25, then shift every letter individually by adding the key to it. So the letter a ROT3 is D. While you're trying to solve these, look for the invariants — what structure stays consistent every time, that you can go hunting for while you try to decrypt?
